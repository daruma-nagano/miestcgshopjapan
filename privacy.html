Privacy & Cookies

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit this website or contact Nexus Trading. It describes this website only — if you buy from us on Whatnot, or message us on Instagram, those platforms handle your data under their own policies as well.

What this website collects

Nothing directly. There is no account system, no order form and no newsletter sign-up on this site, and we run no analytics or advertising scripts of our own. We do not set cookies ourselves and we cannot identify you from a visit.

Services this page loads

Some parts of the page are provided by other companies. When the page opens, your browser contacts them and they receive your IP address and basic request information. They may set their own cookies.

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website and serves every file on it. GitHub records server requests.
  • Google Maps — the shop map is embedded from google.com/maps.
  • Google Fonts — the page's typefaces are served from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com.

If you would rather not load these, most browsers can block third-party content per site. The rest of the page still works without them.

When you contact us

If you message us on Instagram, or call the shop, we use what you send only to answer you and to arrange your order — for example the item you want, your delivery address and your country. We keep it as long as we need it for the order and for our records as a licensed secondhand dealer in Japan. We do not sell it or pass it to anyone other than the shipping and payment providers used for your own order.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at the details below. If you contacted us through a social platform, note that the message history on that platform is also held by the platform, and you may need to ask them separately.
