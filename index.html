How to Buy

Four steps, start to finish

Orders from outside Japan are handled by message, one at a time, so we can confirm stock and quote shipping before you pay.

  1. 01

    Find something you want

    Spot it in an Instagram post, on a Whatnot live, or just ask us what we have from a set you are chasing.

  2. 02

    Message us

    Send a DM on Instagram with the item and your country. Messages are answered in English.

  3. 03

    Get a quote

    We confirm the item is still in stock and reply with the total — item price plus shipping to your address — and the payment methods available for your order.

  4. 04

    Pay, then we ship

    Once payment clears, your order is packed and sent with tracking, and we send you the tracking number.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do you ship outside Japan?

Yes. We ship worldwide from Matsumoto. Rates and delivery times depend on where you are, so message us with your country and we will quote it before you commit.

How much is shipping, and how long does it take?

Both are quoted per order rather than published as a fixed table, because they change with destination, weight and method. Contact us with your address and what you want, and we will confirm the cost and the expected timeframe.

What payment methods can I use?

Available methods depend on your country and the size of the order. We confirm them when we send your quote — please ask before ordering.

Are the cards genuine?

Yes. Everything we sell is Japanese stock, sourced and checked in our own shop in Matsumoto. We also buy cards over the counter, which is where most of our stock comes from.

Can I reserve something I saw on your SNS?

Yes — send a DM as soon as you see it. Stock moves quickly and posts are not automatically updated when an item sells.

Do you have sealed boxes?

Sealed Pokémon Card Game and One Piece Card Game products are a large part of what we carry, subject to availability. Ask about a specific set and we will tell you what is on the shelf.

Do you sell graded cards?

Yes. PSA and other graded cards come through the shop regularly. Tell us what you are looking for and we will let you know when something matches.

Can I visit the shop in person?

Please do. We are about a 7-minute walk from Matsumoto Station, open weekdays 13:00–19:00 and 12:00–19:00 on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. Closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays.

Do you buy cards as well as sell them?

Yes — buying is a large part of what we do, and it is where most of our stock comes from. Current prices for sealed product are on our price list.

What if something arrives damaged?

Contact us as soon as the parcel arrives, with photos of the packaging and the item, and we will work out the next step with you directly.
